## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-clean, engineering-grade storefront for high-end certified refurbished devices, express technical diagnostics, and premium electronics. The identity unites the clinical discipline of high-end hardware manufacturers with the friction-free clarity of flagship direct-to-consumer e-commerce.

### Design Principles
- **Engineering Clarity:** Clean structure, generous spatial margins, and deliberate typography project absolute operational authority and technical competence.
- **Material Restraint:** Color is reserved strictly for interactive affordances, verifiable diagnostic grades, and trust triggers; surfaces rely on micro-delicate luminance differences rather than heavy fills.
- **Physical Fidelity:** Layout cards mirror the physical glass-and-aluminum hardware on display through crisp hairline borders and ambient, diffused elevation.
- **Transactional Confidence:** Instant legibility around battery health, inspection certifications, direct warranty terms, and localized direct-chat conversions (WhatsApp).

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an architectural gallery concept: a soft, light-filtering background canvas supporting pure white interactive modules, anchored by deep zinc typography.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#2563EB`):** Precision Cobalt Blue. Designated for primary call-to-action buttons, checkout triggers, selected filter tabs, and active repair tracking states.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** Electric Azure. Used for diagnostic technical details, secondary badges, specification comparisons, and interactive links.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Precision Emerald. Exclusively reserved for live stock levels, certified multi-point inspection checks, and direct WhatsApp sales/support CTAs.
- **Neutral (`#18181B`):** Zinc-900. High-density core text and monolithic product titles.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBFD` (Clean, warm-tinted off-white avoiding digital eye fatigue).
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure optic white for product cards, modal sheets, and sticky navigation headers).
- **Muted Typography:** `#71717A` (Zinc-500 for secondary technical specs, tax labels, and metadata).
- **Hairline Divider:** `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)` (Subtle separation stroke across cards, tables, and navbars).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples the geometric refinement of Plus Jakarta Sans for titles and numbers with the utilitarian readability of Inter for body text and specifications.

### Typographic Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Use tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to impart an editorial tech feel reminiscent of hardware keynotes.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Set currency values and pricing tables using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in Plus Jakarta Sans Semi-Bold for instant scanning.
- **Technical Specs:** Battery health percentages, storage sizes (e.g., 256GB), and IMEI certification statuses utilize `mono-spec` uppercase treatment with generous letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt spatial grid with a max-width container of `1280px` for catalog views and `1440px` for immersive product presentation pages.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adapters
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margins are locked to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), with gutters at `1rem`. Product cards collapse to 1 or 2 columns based on detail density.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column grid. Margins scale to `2rem`, gutters maintain `1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column grid. Outer margins scale up to `3rem` (`margin`), centering within the maximum boundary. Element gaps follow strict multiples of 4px and 8px.

### Spatial Density
Components leverage generous internal negative space: product showcase cards must feature a minimum of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) of internal padding to isolate high-resolution device photography from text blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying instead on high-precision ambient dispersion and hairline boundary definitions.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#FBFBFD` background.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp border: `1px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Flyouts):** Lifted card state. Border shifts to `1px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Floating Checkout Drawer):** Ultra-translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a bottom border: `1px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs controlled geometric rounding, calibrated to match the physical curvature of contemporary smartphones and tablet displays.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Interactive Elements (Inputs, Standard Buttons, Chips):** `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` (8px–12px).
- **Product & Diagnostic Cards:** `1rem` (16px), maintaining curvature symmetry with inner image containers which use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pill Badges (Condition Grades, Stock Flags):** Fully rounded capsules (`9999px`) for quick visual segregation from structural blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px`, padding `14px 24px`. On hover, subtle luminance lift: `#1D4ED8` with a micro-scale shift of `scale(1.01)`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)`, text `#18181B`. On hover: `#F4F4F5`.
- **WhatsApp Direct CTA:** Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon prefixed, radius `12px`. Used for on-the-spot repair quote confirmation and trade-in valuations.

### Chips & Badges
- **Condition Grade Chips (e.g., "Grade A+ Impecável"):** Soft zinc base `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`, text `#18181B`, radius `9999px`, padding `4px 12px`, font size `12px` semi-bold.
- **Stock & Warranty Badges:** Light emerald tint `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, emerald text `#059669`, with a pulsing 6px green indicator dot.

### Product & Service Cards
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface container, hairline border `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`, `16px` border radius.
- Includes a dedicated top spec bar (e.g., battery health, storage capacity), a centered high-contrast product image on a pure neutral background, and an integrated bottom pricing drawer displaying installment terms prominently.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)`, height `48px`, radius `10px`, padding `0 16px`.
- Focus state: border color `#2563EB`, outer ring `3px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px`, border `1.5px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.2)`. Selected state fills with `#2563EB` containing a crisp white vector checkmark.

### Device Repair Diagnostic Selector
- Modular vertical selector steps enabling users to choose Brand > Device > Fault Type (e.g., "Tela Quebrada", "Troca de Bateria"), displaying real-time estimated turnaround time and starting prices with clean technical iconography.
